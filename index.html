About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the University of Michigan School of Nursing. My research interest is in maternal sleep health and digital health: I study how sleep and circadian characteristics during pregnancy relate to outcomes such as preterm birth and preeclampsia.

Before Michigan, I earned an MPH in Epidemiology and Biostatistics, with a certificate in Artificial Intelligence and Big Data Analytics for Public Health, from Washington University in St. Louis. Earlier, I completed a B.S. in Nursing and a B.A. in Theory and History of Arts at Peking University. I am a Certified in Public Health (CPH) professional.
